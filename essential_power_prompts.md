# Essential Power Prompts for Professionals, Students, and Knowledge Workers

Use these prompts as copy-ready inputs for ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude. Replace bracketed placeholders with your context before running.

## Productivity Boosters Prompts for Faster High-Quality Output

### Prompt #1: Output-Oriented Day Designer
**Prompt:** Act as an execution coach and convert my current workload into an output-first plan for today; my context is [role], my available time is [hours], and my top goals are [goals]. Build a plan with 3 mission-critical outputs, 3 supporting tasks, time estimates, risk blockers, and a hard stop. Return the result as a table with columns: Output, Why It Matters, Timebox, First Step, Completion Criteria.  
**Use-case:** Use this when your day feels busy but unclear. It forces you to define concrete outputs instead of endless task activity.  
**Expected output:** A prioritized daily execution table with explicit deliverables, realistic timeboxes, and objective completion criteria.  
**Tags:** productivity, execution, planning, prioritization, output

### Prompt #2: Task-to-Outcome Converter
**Prompt:** You are a productivity strategist. I will provide a raw task list: [task list]. Convert each task into a business or academic outcome, identify low-value tasks to eliminate, and rewrite remaining items as action statements starting with verbs. Include a final section: "What not to do this week."  
**Use-case:** Use this when your to-do list is long but impact is low. It helps cut busywork and refocus on meaningful outcomes.  
**Expected output:** A cleaned, impact-oriented task list grouped by high, medium, and low strategic value with clear eliminations.  
**Tags:** productivity, leverage, prioritization, 80-20, focus

### Prompt #3: Throughput Bottleneck Finder
**Prompt:** Act as an operations analyst. Analyze my weekly workflow: [workflow steps]. Identify the single biggest throughput bottleneck using constraints thinking, quantify its impact, propose 3 fixes ranked by effort vs impact, and draft a 7-day test plan with metrics.  
**Use-case:** Use this when projects keep slipping despite hard work. It helps identify and fix the true bottleneck instead of optimizing everything.  
**Expected output:** A bottleneck diagnosis, ranked interventions, and a measurable one-week experiment plan.  
**Tags:** productivity, systems, bottlenecks, operations, optimization

### Prompt #4: Context-Switch Cost Reducer
**Prompt:** Act as a focus architect. My current schedule looks like this: [calendar/tasks]. Estimate context-switch overhead, group tasks into deep work, shallow work, and admin batches, and redesign my day to reduce switching by at least 30%. Provide before/after schedule blocks and expected time recovered.  
**Use-case:** Use this when your day is fragmented across tools and meetings. It helps recover lost time from frequent switching.  
**Expected output:** A revised block schedule with grouped task batches and a quantified reduction in switching overhead.  
**Tags:** productivity, deep work, context switching, calendar design, focus

### Prompt #5: 80/20 Leverage Extractor
**Prompt:** You are an 80/20 performance advisor. Given my goals [goals] and current activities [activities], identify the top 20% actions likely to produce 80% of results in the next 30 days. Include stop/start/continue recommendations and one weekly scoreboard.  
**Use-case:** Use this when effort is high but progress feels flat. It surfaces compounding actions and removes low-leverage activity.  
**Expected output:** A 30-day leverage plan with top actions, de-prioritized work, and a simple weekly performance scoreboard.  
**Tags:** productivity, 80-20, leverage, performance, weekly review

## Decision Making Prompts for Better Trade-Offs and Lower Regret

### Prompt #11: Trade-Off Matrix Builder
**Prompt:** You are a strategic decision advisor. Evaluate options [option A], [option B], [option C] against criteria [criteria list]. **Use-case:** Use this when choices are complex and emotionally noisy. It structures decisions around explicit criteria and assumptions.  
**Expected output:** A weighted comparison table, assumption audit, and recommendation with decision confidence notes.  
**Tags:** decision making, trade-offs, matrix, strategy, analysis

### Prompt #12: Reversible vs Irreversible Decision Test
**Prompt:** Act as a risk analyst. For this decision [decision], classify which parts are reversible vs irreversible, estimate downside if wrong, propose a low-risk pilot for reversible components, and define escalation triggers before full commitment.  
**Use-case:** Use this before high-stakes commitments. It prevents over-committing where staged testing is possible.  
**Expected output:** A decision breakdown with pilot path, risk boundaries, and escalation criteria.  
**Tags:** risk management, reversible decisions, experimentation, strategy, governance

### Prompt #13: Pre-Mortem Risk Scanner

**Use-case:** Use this before launching projects, programs, or study plans. It identifies risks while they are still controllable.  
**Expected output:** A practical risk register including failure scenarios, warning signals, owners, and mitigation actions.  
**Tags:** pre-mortem, risk register, planning, project management, controls

### Prompt #14: Opportunity Cost Calculator
**Prompt:** You are a strategic planner. Given this proposal [proposal] and alternatives [alternatives], calculate opportunity costs across time, money, learning, and reputation. Provide a decision memo with short-term and long-term consequences, including "cost of inaction."

**Expected output:** A concise opportunity-cost memo with explicit trade-offs and recommendation logic.  
**Tags:** opportunity cost, strategic thinking, decision memo, long-term planning, trade-offs

### Prompt #15: One-Page Decision Memo Draft
**Prompt:** Act as my chief strategy writer. Draft a one-page decision memo for [decision topic] using this structure: Context, Problem, Options, Decision Criteria, Recommendation, Risks, Mitigations, 30-Day Review Plan. Keep the tone neutral and evidence-based.
