## Extracted patterns and adaptation
- Apple HIG: prioritize hierarchy, harmony, and consistency.
  - Applied as clearer section rhythm, intentional card hierarchy, and cleaner CTA ladder on homepage.
- Apple accessibility guidance: design for perceivable/adaptable interactions.
  - Applied through reduced-motion handling, keyboard-first controls, and stronger search/result semantics.
- Medium guidance: content quality depends on clear headlines, formatting, and image utility.
  - Applied to article panel metadata readability, content-map progression, and structured related surfaces.
- Notion model: template-first discoverability and scalable information architecture.
  - Applied through resources hub filters, suggestion chips, and modular card templates.
- Jeff Su model: high-clarity personal positioning + newsletter conversion + practical offers.
  - Applied by adding homepage brand story and stronger deep-page CTA sequencing.
- iPhoneLife model: category-led discovery with deep guides and recurring educational assets.
  - Applied to featured categories, resources learning paths, and utility-first navigation.
- Behance model: visual card grids with concise context and scan-friendly previews.
  - Applied to resource card styling, search-result cards, and hub recommendation cards.
