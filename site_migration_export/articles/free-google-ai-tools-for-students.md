# Free Google AI Tools for Students (Here’s How to Get Them)

Free Google AI Tools for Students are now available, and this could be a game-changer for learners in 2025. If you’re a college student in India, Google is giving you completely free access to its most powerful AI tools — normally priced at ₹19,500 per year.

From essay writing to video creation, these tools can help you learn, build, and grow smarter. And the best part? You don’t need to spend a single rupee.

Offer ends on September 15, 2025

## Transform Your Learning Experience

Google is offering students free access to its premium AI bundle, normally priced at ₹19,500 a year. We’re talking advanced tools like Gemini 2.5 Pro, Vivo3 for instant video creation, and even AI-powered Google Docs and Gmail.

Let’s face it — learning today isn’t what it used to be. From writing essays to building creative projects or crunching data, students are juggling a lot. Now imagine having access to some of Google’s most powerful AI tools — totally free. No catch. Just smart tech helping you get things done faster.

## What Exactly Are You Getting?

This isn’t a watered-down version — it’s the real deal. Here’s what comes with the student plan:

### Gemini 2.5 Pro

Google’s top-tier AI assistant that can:

- Help you fix code
- Draft that last-minute assignment
- Prep for tests or interviews
- Give you writing suggestions that actually make sense

### Vivo3 – Make Videos from Text or Images

Drop in an idea or an image, and it’ll whip up a photo-realistic 8-second video in seconds. Perfect for:

- School projects
- Content creation
- YouTube shorts

### NotebookLM

Not great at note-taking? No problem. This tool pulls key insights from your reading material and can even turn them into audio — like your own study podcast.

### Deep Research AI

Working on a research paper? This tool helps:

- Organize ideas quickly
- Find credible sources
- Build strong arguments

### 2TB of Google Cloud Storage

No more deleting files or running out of space. You’ll have plenty of room for documents, PDFs, videos — all backed up and synced.

### Gemini in Google Workspace

Gemini AI now works directly inside:

- Docs – write faster
- Sheets – analyze smarter
- Gmail – email like a pro
- Slides – polish up presentations

### Visc Animate + VO3 Fast

These tools let you animate still images and turn them into short, eye-catching videos. Creative students? You’ll love this.

### Bonus: 1,000 AI Credits Monthly

Use these on Visc, Flow, and other platforms that support AI content creation. Extra resources to fuel your creativity every month.

Be 18 or older
