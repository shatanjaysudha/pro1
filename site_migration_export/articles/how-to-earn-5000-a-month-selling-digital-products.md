# How to Earn $5000 a Month Selling Digital Products

It’s not a dream—it’s a proven reality. In this blog, I’ll walk you through the exact strategies I use to generate monthly income selling ebooks, templates, and more.

No inventory, no shipping—just pure digital success.

Hey! Over the last three years, I’ve turned digital products into a reliable income stream that brings me over $5,000 every month—all while working less than 20 hours a week.

Today, I want to show you 10 awesome digital products you can start selling to make extra income—or even turn into your main business. You don’t need a huge following, and you’re not limited to just Etsy. These ideas can work on their own or even better when combined.

Some of them can become passive—meaning you put in the work once, and they keep earning for you again and again.

## Why Choose Digital Products?

Digital products are items people buy and download instantly. Think ebooks, photos, design files, templates, or online classes.

They’re awesome because:

### No Inventory Needed

You don’t need to hold physical stock or manage warehouse space.

### No Packaging or Shipping

Say goodbye to packaging materials, shipping costs, and delivery delays.

### Super Low Costs

After initial creation, your profit margins are incredibly high.

### Sell 24/7 Globally

Your digital store is always open to customers around the world.

### Create Once, Sell Forever

Make it once and enjoy ongoing revenue with minimal maintenance.

### Growing Demand

Especially in online learning, design, and content creation.

## 10 Digital Products You Can Sell to Hit $5K/Month

Each of these products has the potential to become a significant income stream when executed properly.

1

### Sell Photos and Videos Online

If you’re good with a camera or phone, your photos and video clips can earn you money. Businesses, bloggers, and marketers are always searching for original visuals.

Upload your work to platforms like Adobe Stock, Getty, or Shutterstock, and earn a payout each time your file is downloaded.

Adobe Stock
Getty
Shutterstock

![Photographer selling digital photos online](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1516035069371-29a1b244cc32?ixlib=rb-4.0.3&auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

2

### Turn Your Art Into Digital Products

Create illustrations, digital paintings, or custom graphics? You can offer them as downloads or prints.

Sites like Etsy, Redbubble, or Creative Market make it easy to get started. People buy digital art for websites, music covers, gifts, and home decor every day.

Etsy
Redbubble
Creative Market

![Digital artist creating and selling artwork](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1541961017774-22349e4a1262?ixlib=rb-4.0.3&auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

3

### Create and Sell Audio Files

From sound effects to music loops, or even calming meditations—audio is a hot-selling category.

If you’re into music production or recording, upload your audio to AudioJungle, Pond5, or build a niche site for your files. It’s a great fit for YouTubers, editors, and creators who need fresh audio.

AudioJungle
Pond5

![Audio producer creating sound effects and music](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1505740420928-5e560c06d30e?ixlib=rb-4.0.3&auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

4

### Write an eBook

If you enjoy writing or have something useful to share—like tips, tutorials, or life experiences—why not create an eBook?

Tools like Canva and Google Docs make design easy, and you can sell through Amazon KDP or your own site. To boost sales, consider adding extras like bonus guides or audio versions.

Canva
Google Docs
Amazon KDP

![Author writing and selling an ebook](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1495640388908-05fa85288e61?ixlib=rb-4.0.3&auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

5

### Build an Online Course

This one changed everything for me. Teaching something you know can become a high-earning digital product.

Courses can be short or long, and sold for anywhere between $20 and $1,000+ depending on your topic. You can host them on platforms like Kajabi, Teachable, or directly on your site for full control and better profit.

Kajabi
Teachable

![Instructor creating an online course](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1584697964358-3e14ca57658b?ixlib=rb-4.0.3&auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

6

### Sell Custom Templates

Templates help people save time. Think resume designs, business card layouts, Instagram post formats, or content calendars.

Design in Canva or Illustrator, and list your files on Gumroad or Etsy. They’re easy to create and can sell hundreds of times.

Canva
Illustrator
Gumroad
Etsy

![Designer creating and selling digital templates](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1552664730-d307ca884978?ixlib=rb-4.0.3&auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

7

### Offer Product Bundles

Got more than one product? Group them together and offer a discount. Bundles make your offer more attractive and can boost revenue quickly.

For example, if you sell a $30 ebook, a $50 course, and a $20 workbook, sell them together for $79 as a limited-time deal. People love value-packed offers.

![Digital product bundles with discount offer](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1607082350899-7e105aa886ae?ixlib=rb-4.0.3&auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

8

### Promote Products as an Affiliate

Even if you don’t create anything, you can earn money by recommending other people’s products.

Affiliate marketing is simple: share your referral link, and earn when someone buys through it. Programs like Amazon Associates, ClickBank, and tool-based programs like Kajabi can bring in recurring income each month.

Amazon Associates
ClickBank
Kajabi

![Affiliate marketer promoting digital products](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1551836022-d5d88e9218df?ixlib=rb-4.0.3&auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

9

### Start a Paid Membership

Offer exclusive content, live sessions, behind-the-scenes videos, or access to a private community for a monthly fee.

With just 100 members paying $50/month, that’s $5,000/month right there. Platforms like Circle or Skool are easy to set up and manage.

Circle
Skool

![Online community and paid membership site](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1552668666-8709c9c50a35?ixlib=rb-4.0.3&auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

10

### Create a Tool or Software

If you’re tech-savvy (or can partner with someone who is), building a simple app or tool can generate serious income.

Think of a problem people face, and build a solution for it. Offer free versions with premium upgrades. You can list tools on CodeCanyon, or sell subscriptions directly on your website.

CodeCanyon

![Developer creating a digital tool or software](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1555066931-4365d14bab8c?ixlib=rb-4.0.3&auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## Wrapping Up

Each of these ideas can bring you closer to your goal—whether that’s $1,000/month or a full $5,000+.

Start simple. Pick one thing. Test and improve. And once it’s working, you can scale or add more products to increase your income.

If this helped, feel free to leave a comment or share which idea you want to try first.
