# How to Earn ₹8000 Per Day Online: The Complete Guide

Based on a proven method by The Side Hustle Guy, this strategy works for beginners with just a laptop and internet

## The Opportunity in 2025

With increasing unemployment and rising living costs, many people are looking for ways to earn money online. What if you could make ₹8000 every single day—without any office, boss, or fixed timings—using just a laptop and internet?

![Person working remotely from home](https://images.pexels.com/photos/3183153/pexels-photo-3183153.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1260&h=750&dpr=1)

This is exactly what The Side Hustle Guy shares in his latest video. In this comprehensive guide, we’ll break down his strategy step by step, and show how you can start earning from Day 1.

### Who is The Side Hustle Guy?

He’s a YouTuber who specializes in real, honest side hustle methods for Indians. He doesn’t promote scams, paid courses, or unrealistic “get rich quick” schemes. Instead, he shares systems that work for freelancers, students, part-timers, and even full-timers.

## The Proven 6-Step Strategy

This strategy has helped thousands of people across India make a consistent income through simple freelancing, content writing, and outreach—all done using just a laptop.

1

### Pick ONE High-Demand Skill

He emphasizes the power of specialization. Choose one skill that:

- Is in high demand online
- You enjoy or are willing to learn
- Doesn’t require a degree

**Examples:** Resume Writing, Canva Design, Social Media Content, Product Descriptions, Translation

**Tip:** Learn these skills from free resources like YouTube or Skillshare.

2

### Build Sample Work

Before approaching clients, create 2-3 “fake” projects to demonstrate your skills:

- Instagram post mockups in Canva
- Sample resumes
- Portfolio PDFs using free tools

**Tools used:** Canva, Google Docs, Behance/Google Drive

3

### Set Up Your Online Profile

Start on freelancing platforms that don’t require money to join:

- Fiverr
- Upwork
- Freelancer.com
- Truelancer (India-based)

Create a profile with professional photo, clear skill description, and 2-3 pricing options.

4

### Client Outreach Strategy

Send 10-15 outreach messages daily using:

- LinkedIn (small business owners)
- Instagram (DM pages)
- Cold emails
- Fiverr buyer requests

**Hack:** Use pre-written templates and customize names/brands to save time.

5

### Close Deals & Deliver Fast

When clients respond:

- Send a quick, polished draft
- Overdeliver (add bonus tips/designs)
- Use templates & AI tools to work faster

**Average project value:** ₹800-₹2500

**Target:** 3-5 projects/day = ₹8000+

6

### Scale with Systems

Once your system works:

- Reuse templates to save time
- Build a team or hire junior freelancers
- Create Standard Operating Procedures

After 50+ clients, you’ll get repeat work and referrals making the system automated.
