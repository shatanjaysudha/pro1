# Agentic AI Roadmap 2025: From Beginner to Advanced AI Builder

Just know basic Python? That’s all you need. This roadmap will take you the rest of the way.

## What Is Agentic AI?

Agentic AI refers to AI systems that can **think, plan, and act independently** — not just answer prompts, but **use tools**, remember context, and complete multi-step tasks like digital employees.

![Agentic AI Roadmap 2025 - AI systems thinking and planning](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1677442135722-5f8ea49cec8a?ixlib=rb-4.0.3&auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

Agentic AI systems can perform complex, multi-step tasks independently

But here’s the big question: “How do I start if I don’t have a background in Machine Learning?”

Enter the **Agentic AI Roadmap 2025** — a completely free learning path by Dhaval Patel (aka @DhavalSays), designed to help anyone go from beginner to advanced AI builder.

## Roadmap Breakdown: Step-by-Step

1

### Core Foundations

Build a solid understanding of the fundamentals:

- Python fundamentals (quick recap)
- Data structures & algorithms
- Statistics and probability for AI
- Deep learning & neural networks
- Transformers & attention mechanisms

**Goal:** Build intuition, not just memorize formulas.

2

### Generative AI Concepts

Dive into modern AI approaches:

- Large Language Models (LLMs) explained
- Prompt engineering techniques
- Vector embeddings & similarity search
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

**All with practical examples and beginner-friendly code.**

![Generative AI Concepts in Agentic AI Roadmap](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1677442135133-33d364e7d2d1?ixlib=rb-4.0.3&auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

Understanding generative AI is crucial for building advanced AI systems

3

### Agentic Frameworks & Tools

Start building functional AI systems:

- Introduction to LangChain & LangGraph
- Build simple AI agents using Agno
- Design stateful agents with LangGraph
- Add memory, reasoning, and tool use to your AI workflows

**This is where you stop using AI… and start building it.**

4

### MCP (Multi-Component Processor) Architecture

Create sophisticated AI systems:

- Learn how to build complex, multi-functional AI systems
- Set up your first MCP server
- Combine planning, memory, tools, and self-correction in one flow

**Ideal for advanced builders looking to create real-world systems.**

![MCP Architecture in Agentic AI Roadmap](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1677442135699-3d09b4e9a1e2?ixlib=rb-4.0.3&auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

MCP Architecture enables complex AI systems to work together seamlessly
