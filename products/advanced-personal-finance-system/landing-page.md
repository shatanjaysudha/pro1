# Advanced Personal Finance System

**Subhead:** A premium multi-year Google Sheets finance engine with automation, KPI dashboards, forecasting, and sell-ready structure.

## What this template is
This is a complex personal finance operating system designed for serious tracking and decision-making. It combines transaction capture, category intelligence, monthly/annual summaries, net-worth growth, and forecast alerts in one coherent framework.

## Built for
- Professionals managing high transaction volume
- Families tracking budgets across multiple goals
- Creators selling premium finance templates
- Operators who want automated insight, not manual reporting

## Included sheets (10)
1. Dashboard
2. Income Register
3. Expense Register
4. Cash Flow Tracker
5. Savings & Goals
6. Monthly Summaries
7. Category & Trend Charts
8. Net Worth Tracker
9. Forecasts & Alerts
10. Instructions & Branding

## Core capabilities
- Year-over-year KPI cards
- Auto year/month extraction using ARRAYFORMULA
- Duplicate checks in income logs
- Category-level budget utilization alerts
- Cash flow projection and moving averages
- Net worth timeline with YoY delta
- Forecast warning engine for savings and cash-flow risk
- Named ranges + data validation + warning-only protections

## Delivery files
- `personal-finance-system-builder.gs`
- `INSTRUCTIONS.md`
- `FORMULAS.md`
- `QUALITY-CHECK.md`

## Setup
Paste Apps Script into a Google Sheet and run one function to create all 10 tabs with formulas, formatting, validations, and charts.
